# Privacy Policy for Lumen Harbor (iOS)

**Effective date:** 2026-10-22

Lumen Harbor ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Lumen Harbor iOS app handles information.

## Information We Collect

At this time, Lumen Harbor does not require account creation and does not directly collect personally identifiable information from users.

Depending on your device settings and Apple services, Apple may process limited technical data (such as diagnostics or crash information) to operate and improve platform services.

## How Information Is Used

Any technical data available to us is used only to:

- Maintain app reliability and performance
- Diagnose crashes and fix bugs
- Improve app functionality

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

## Data Retention

If diagnostic information is received, it is retained only as long as needed to investigate issues and improve the app.

## Children's Privacy

Lumen Harbor is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your Choices

You can control diagnostic and analytics sharing through your iOS device settings.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted in this repository with a revised effective date.
