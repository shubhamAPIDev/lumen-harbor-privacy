# Privacy Policy — Lumen Harbor

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026  
**Last updated:** October 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Lumen Harbor (“the App”) is a Christian prayer application that includes Catholic Rosary prayers and related spiritual features. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

The App is developed by **Shubham Gupta** (“I,” “me,” or “the Developer”).

The App is designed to provide its core prayer features offline, without requiring you to create an account or register.

## 2. Information the App Stores

The App may store the following information locally on your device to provide its features:

- **Prayer intentions and notes:** Intentions you choose to save and any associated notes.
- **Prayer progress:** Records of prayers completed and related progress information.
- **App preferences:** Settings and preferences needed to personalize your experience.
- **Widget information:** Relevant app data shared locally with the App’s widget through Apple’s App Group functionality.

This information is stored using Apple’s on-device storage mechanisms, such as UserDefaults and the App Group shared container. The App does not require you to submit this information to an online account or developer-operated server to use its core prayer features.

## 3. Apple Health and Mindful Minutes

The App includes an optional feature that can record mindful prayer sessions in Apple Health using Apple’s HealthKit framework.

If you choose to use this feature, the App requests permission to **write mindful-session data** to Apple Health. The App does not request permission to read HealthKit data.

You can grant or revoke this permission through Apple’s Health or device settings. If you do not grant permission, the HealthKit-related functionality may not be available, but you can continue using the App’s other features.

Health information is sensitive. You should review Apple’s privacy information and Health permissions to understand how Apple Health stores and manages your information.

## 4. Internet Connectivity, Analytics, and Advertising

The App’s core prayer features are designed to work offline and do not require an online account.

The App is not intended to sell personal information or use personal information for targeted advertising. It does not require third-party advertising or analytics services for its core functionality.

Your device’s operating system and other services you independently use may process information according to their own settings and policies. For example, Apple may process information associated with device backups or system services.

## 5. Device Backups and Data Retention

Information stored locally by the App may be included in device backups, depending on your device settings and Apple’s backup behavior. This Privacy Policy does not guarantee that locally stored information is excluded from iCloud or other device backups.

Your saved intentions, prayer progress, and preferences generally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the relevant app data, or remove the App. Uninstalling the App may remove locally stored information, but information included in a backup may remain in that backup.

You are responsible for managing your device backups and deciding whether to retain or delete them.

## 6. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable prayer reminders, the App may use Apple’s local notification functionality to deliver notifications on your device.

You can manage or disable notifications through your device settings. The App does not require you to enable reminders to use its core prayer features.

## 7. Information You Send by Email

If you contact me at **hematiterosary@gmail.com**, I will receive the information you choose to include in your message, such as your email address, the contents of your message, and any attachments.

I use this information to respond to your inquiry, provide support, and address issues relating to the App. Your email provider may also process your correspondence under its own privacy policy.

Please avoid including sensitive personal or health information in support emails unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

## 8. Sharing and Selling of Information

I do not sell your personal information.

The App’s core features are designed to operate without sending your prayer intentions, notes, or prayer progress to a developer-operated server. I do not intentionally disclose such locally stored information to third parties through the App.

Information you choose to share by email is processed as described in Section 7. Apple and other service providers may separately process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 9. Data Security

Information stored locally is subject to the security protections provided by your device and its operating system. You can help protect it by keeping your device updated, using a device passcode, and managing access to your device and backups.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including the right to request access, correction, or deletion, to object to certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Because the App’s core prayer information is stored locally on your device, you can generally manage or delete that information directly through the App or by removing its stored data, where those options are available.

If you have a privacy question or wish to exercise a right that applies to information I hold, contact me using the details in Section 13. I may need to verify your request before responding.

If you are in the European Economic Area, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection supervisory authority.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not designed to collect personal information from children through its core prayer features. It does not require an account or registration.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided to me by email and should be addressed, please contact me using the details below.
