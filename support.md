# Lumen Harbor Support

**A quieter place to pray.**

Welcome to Lumen Harbor Support. If you have a question, encounter a problem, or want to share feedback about the app, I'm here to help.

## How can we help?

### Prayer Intentions

Lumen Harbor lets you create personal prayer intentions and track them as you pray. If you experience an issue with adding, viewing, or checking off intentions, please contact me.

### Holy Rosary

If you experience a problem while using the Rosary prayer features, please include a brief description of what happened.

### Offline Access

Lumen Harbor is designed to provide its core prayer features offline. If a feature isn't working as expected, please let me know which feature you were using.

### Privacy

Your personal prayer intentions are designed to remain on your device. For information about data handling and privacy, please read the [Lumen Harbor Privacy Policy](https://shubhamapidev.github.io/lumen-harbor-privacy/).

## Contact Support
